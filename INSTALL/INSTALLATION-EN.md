# Installing WinGuard

Hi, and thanks for trying **WinGuard** 🙂

WinGuard is a free app that helps you take back control of your privacy on Windows 11: telemetry, ads, background services phoning home... you tick a box, you apply, and you're done.

Here's how to install it, step by step, no stress.

## What you need

- Windows 11 (any edition)
- Administrator rights on your PC (so it can change system settings)

## Installation (the easy way)

1. Go to the **Releases** section of this repo and download **`WinGuardSetup.exe`**.
2. Double-click it.
3. Windows may show a blue SmartScreen warning because the app isn't signed with a paid certificate yet. That's normal: click **"More info"**, then **"Run anyway"**.
4. Follow the installer, then launch WinGuard.

That's it, you're ready.

## How to use it

1. Open WinGuard.
2. **Before changing anything**, the first time, keep **"Create a restore point before applying"** ticked: that way, if you don't like a tweak, you can roll back with peace of mind.
3. Tick the protections you want (or pick a ready-made profile).
4. Click **Apply**. Done.

Your settings stay active even after you close the app or reboot: WinGuard doesn't need to run in the background.

## Undoing everything

Just click **"Revert all"** in the app, or untick a specific tweak and re-apply. You can also use the restore point created at the start.

## One last thing

WinGuard is a project built with care to respect your privacy 100%. If you hit a bug or have an idea, feel free to open an *issue* on GitHub.
